Key insights

What Will This Financial Checklist Help You See?

1
Cash visibility

Are sales actually turning into cash?

See whether cash is tied up in payouts, refunds, receivables, suppliers or expenses.

2
Margin clarity

Are your products really as profitable as they look?

Look beyond selling price and consider the costs involved in getting products to customers.

3
Inventory visibility

Do you know what your stock is really worth?

Spot inventory that may be sitting in damaged, expired, promotional or slow-moving stock.

4
Tax & reporting readiness

Could your records stand up to scrutiny?

Identify gaps that may need attention before VAT or Corporate Tax reporting.
